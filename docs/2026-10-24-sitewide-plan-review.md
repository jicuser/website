## Deliberate boundaries

The plan's restricted support-case service needs confirmed staffing, access/retention rules and a consent/referral process before activation. No general inbox or existing safeguarding/DBS module is relabelled as that service. Anonymous guidance does not create a case. A complete revision-history/approval system for all historical editors and arbitrary per-section design controls are also not claimed. Existing section reorder, visibility, content/image editing and actual save boundaries remain; the new appearance workspace supplies the explicitly supported design controls.

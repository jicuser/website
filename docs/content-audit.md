# Public website copy audit

Reviewed 18 October 2026 in `/workspace/jic-website`. This covers public source copy, local defaults and visitor-facing account screens. Live CMS rows, private account records and external channel content were not fetched or edited. The separate preproduction site was not changed.

The copy now describes a real Islamic centre and gives visitors a clear next step. Existing contact details, historical dates, financial figures, poster transcriptions, religious text, CMS keys and publication controls were preserved. No software subscription, payroll, automatic attendance, recording or payment-automation claims were added.

## Hosted content and factual follow-up

The following were not inspected live and may override or add to the reviewed source:

- `page_content` values, including `page:*` titles/bodies, navigation visibility, homepage text, notice/reminder collections, posters, WhatsApp settings and `masjid_project_stages`.
- Published `page_sections`, pages returned by `list_site_pages` / `get_site_page`, and public forms returned by `get_public_form`, including every staff-authored field label and description.
- Prayer timetable/Jummah records, announcements, events, livestream settings, hall bookings and team profiles.
- Public campaign stories, organiser names, totals, issued invoices, certificates, course materials, learning records, replies and reviewed talk publications.
- Content delivered by Google Maps, AlQuran Cloud, social platforms, radio, Polycam or linked publishers, and text within externally managed or newly uploaded images.

The supplied poster dates, fees and contact information were preserved rather than independently reverified. The centre should confirm current programme availability, admissions, opening times, access arrangements, project status and any additional CMS claims before publication. This is a content dependency, not a change to staff publication permissions.

The privacy page now reflects the source implementation. The centre still needs to confirm its operational lawful bases, retention periods, processor arrangements, safeguarding policy and handling of activity health information. This audit does not certify those policies or claim that hosted settings have been checked.
